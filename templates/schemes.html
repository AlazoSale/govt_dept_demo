Scheme record - SWD-02 Widow & Destitute Women PensionInfo

Edit record · Upload guideline / GO
Objective
Monthly pension assistance to widows and destitute women aged 18-59 from below-poverty-line households, to secure minimum subsistence support.
Eligibility
Widow or destitute woman, age 18-59, BPL household, not receiving any other statutory pension.
Funding pattern
State Plan, 100% state share. Budget head 2235-02-103.
Implementing agency
District Social Welfare Offices; sanction by Block Development Officer; disbursement through DBT.
Coverage
23 districts, 318 blocks.
Beneficiaries (Nov 2026)
41,208 active; 1,142 applications pending sanction.
