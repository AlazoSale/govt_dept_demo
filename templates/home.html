About Us

We offer a unique coffee house environment unlike any other in Texas.

Born in a tiny roastery on Pecan Street, Abilene Coffee Shop grew from one drum roaster and a stubborn belief: great coffee should feel like coming home. Today our baristas still taste every batch, steam every pitcher by hand, and know most regulars by name — and by order.

  • Small-batch beans roasted in-house every single morning
  • Cozy corners, fast Wi-Fi & a shaded patio made for slow days
  • Pastries & breakfast bites from local Abilene bakers
